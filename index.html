💻 exynos9810-woa - Run Windows 11 on Your Galaxy S9+

This project brings the full Windows 11 desktop to the Samsung Galaxy S9+ (model SM-G965F, Exynos 9810). It includes the boot firmware, the hardware drivers and an installer that takes your phone from stock Android to a working Windows desktop — no programming knowledge required on your side.

📦 What Is exynos9810-woa?

Your Galaxy S9+ is a small, powerful computer. Underneath Android it has an ARM processor, memory, storage, a screen and a battery. Windows 11 also runs on ARM processors, which is why the two can meet in the middle.

exynos9810-woa is the bridge between them. It is a bundle of three things working together:

  • UEFI firmware — the small startup program that tells your phone how to hand control over to Windows instead of Android.
  • Drivers — the pieces of software that let Windows understand your phone's screen, touch panel, buttons, storage, battery charging and wireless hardware.
  • An installer — a guided, point-and-click tool that runs on your Windows PC and handles the entire process for you.

You do not edit files, type commands or build anything. You download, you launch the installer, you follow the on-screen prompts, and your phone boots into the Windows desktop.

🖥️ Before You Begin

Take two minutes to check that you have everything below. This prevents almost every problem people run into.

  • A Samsung Galaxy S9+ with model number SM-G965F (the Exynos 9810 version). The package is built specifically for this model.
  • A Windows 10 or Windows 11 computer with a free USB port and at least 15 GB of free disk space.
  • A good quality USB-C cable that carries data, not just power. A charging-only cable will not work.
  • A charged phone battery — at least 60% before you start.
  • A backup of your personal data. The process replaces the operating system, so photos, messages and accounts on the phone will be erased. Copy anything important to your PC or to cloud storage first.

🚀 Getting Started

The whole process is a short sequence of clicks. Read the steps once before you begin, then work through them in order.

Step 1 — Download the package. Visit the releases page and download the latest version listed at the top. The newest entry is always the recommended one.

Step 2 — Unpack it on your PC. The download arrives as a compressed archive. Right-click it and choose Extract All, then pick a folder you can easily find, such as your Desktop or Downloads folder. Do not run anything from inside the compressed file itself.

Step 3 — Open the installer. Inside the extracted folder you will find the installer application. Double-click it. If Windows shows a security prompt asking whether to allow the app to make changes, choose Yes.

Step 4 — Connect your phone. Switch the phone off, then hold the correct button combination to enter download mode when the installer asks for it. The installer displays the exact buttons on screen at that moment, so there is nothing to memorise.

Step 5 — Let it run. The installer checks the device, prepares storage, writes the firmware and copies the drivers. A progress bar shows each stage. Do not unplug the cable until the installer tells you it has finished.

Step 6 — First boot. When the installer says the process is complete, disconnect the cable and restart the phone. Windows will begin its own first-run setup: pick your language, region and network, then land on the desktop.

🔧 What Happens During Setup

Knowing what to expect makes the process far less stressful. Here is what the installer actually does, in plain terms:

  1. Device check. It confirms your phone is an SM-G965F and reads its current firmware state.
  2. Preparation. It clears space for Windows and sets up the partition layout the new system needs.
  3. Firmware write. It installs the UEFI startup layer so the phone knows to launch Windows instead of Android.
  4. System copy. It transfers the Windows image and the driver set to the internal storage.
  5. Verification. It re-reads what it wrote and confirms nothing was corrupted in transit.
  6. Finish. It reports success and tells you to restart the phone.

Depending on your PC's speed and your cable, the full sequence usually finishes in well under an hour. Leave the phone connected throughout — an interrupted transfer is the single most common cause of a failed setup.

❓ Common Questions

Is this safe to try?

It is a modification of your phone's operating system, so it is not risk-free in the way that installing an app is. That said, the installer performs checks before it changes anything, and the release documentation includes a restore path back to stock Android. Back up your data first and follow the steps exactly.

Will I lose Android forever?

No. The restore procedure is documented in each release. You can return to Android at any time, and many people switch back and forth to compare.

Do I need a developer account or special tools?

No account is needed. The installer is a normal Windows application. Nothing extra has to be installed beforehand.

My cable is fine for charging — is that enough?

Not always. Some cables only carry power. If the installer cannot see the phone, try a different cable first; it solves most connection issues.

Can I use a different Galaxy model?

This package is built for the SM-G965F with the Exynos 9810 chip. Other models and other chips use different hardware and are not supported by these drivers.

How do I know I have the right version?

Open Settings on Android, go to About phone, and look for the model number. It must read SM-G965F.

🛟 Troubleshooting

  • The installer does not detect my phone. Swap the USB cable, try a different USB port — ideally one directly on the PC rather than a hub — and restart the installer.
  • The extraction fails. You may have an incomplete download. Delete the archive, download it again from the releases page, and extract to a folder with a short path such as C:\woa.
  • Windows shows a warning when launching the installer. This is the standard prompt for downloaded applications. Choose More info and then Run anyway.
  • The phone boots but shows a blank screen. Hold the power button until it restarts. If the same thing happens, re-run the installer and keep the cable connected for the entire verification stage.
  • Touch input feels offset. Open Windows display settings and confirm the recommended resolution is selected; the driver calibrates against it.
